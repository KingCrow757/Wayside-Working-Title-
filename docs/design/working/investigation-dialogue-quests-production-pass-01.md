# Wayside — Investigation, Dialogue & Quests Production Pass 01

**Status:** Working proposal; not canon.
**Shared dependencies:** Narrative spine, community knowledge, companions, Progression knowledge tags.

## 1. Decision

Investigation is the process that turns a visible road problem into informed, morally distinct project options. It uses authored evidence, deterministic access, and explicit inference summaries. It is not a detective sandbox or a universal dialogue-stat simulator.

## 2. Investigation grammar

Each substantial investigation uses at most:

- 1 presenting problem;
- 3–5 evidence nodes across people, place, and record/material;
- 1 optional corroboration;
- 2–3 conclusions that unlock or reframe project/quest approaches;
- 1 decision whose uncertainty is ethical or informational, not a hidden dice roll.

Critical conclusions require either two ordinary evidence nodes or one decisive node. Knowledge, tools, backgrounds, and companions reveal alternate evidence or interpretation; they do not automatically pronounce the correct moral answer.

The journal separates **Observed**, **Reported**, and **Inferred** information and cites its source. Players may pin a current lead and receive written directions/search areas. Exact markers appear only with exact knowledge; guidance assists can add them without reward penalty.

## 3. Dialogue model

Use short branching conversations with authored intents. Ordinary conversations target 3–6 player selections; major confrontations may reach 10–14 across phases. Deterministic requirement tags display the known missing category, not hidden numbers.

Dialogue consequences write shared memories or quest states. Do not store every line choice. Repeated ambient conversations collapse to a current-state summary plus a small rotating line set.

The silent protagonist has authored anchors and selectable outlook tones—Practical, Patient, Forthright—used as phrasing, not morality or personality statistics. Background and knowledge choices appear only where they change meaning.

## 4. Quest state contract

Every quest is a data-authored finite state machine with explicit entry, active steps, transformed/failure states, and resolution. Most missed opportunities transform once; they do not spawn endless recovery branches. Hard deadlines are rare, named in time blocks, and receive a warning before commitment.

Budget ownership:

- 6 major project/covenant chains;
- 6–8 substantial side investigations;
- 12 micro-stories/incidents;
- 2 companion chains;
- main plot beats embedded in the major chains.

A quest must serve at least two of: road state, community, covenant, companion, region, or player approach. Otherwise cut or merge it.

## 5. References and philosophy

- **Return of the Obra Dinn:** borrow evidence synthesis and player inference; reject a single giant deduction grid and exhaustive identity puzzle.
- **Pentiment:** borrow testimony shaped by position and incomplete truth; reject dialogue/content scale and hard schedule pressure.
- **Disco Elysium:** borrow requirements that reveal distinct interpretations; reject randomized checks and the enormous skill-voice matrix.
- **Roadwarden:** borrow concise journal directions and consequences embedded in routes; reject text-only encounters as the dominant presentation.
- **Fail-forward design:** failure should produce a comprehensible changed problem, but transformation is authored once—not infinite branching.

## 7. Gates

- **I1 comprehension:** after one five-node case, players accurately distinguish fact, testimony, and inference.
- **I2 solution causality:** players can explain which evidence unlocked each project option.
- **I3 controller journal:** reach pinned lead, source, and next direction in three navigation actions from gameplay.
- **I4 authoring reproduction:** build a second investigation from the schema without code changes.
- **I5 transformed failure:** test one missed time block; changed content must remain meaningful and cheaper than an independent quest branch.

No gate has been tested. Exact UI, word counts, and hint escalation remain Open.

## 8. Scope exclusions

No LLM dialogue, procedural cases, universal interrogation system, randomized persuasion, voiced dialogue, cinematic conversation cameras, every-NPC schedules, quest generator, endless radiant incidents, or online collaborative deduction.
