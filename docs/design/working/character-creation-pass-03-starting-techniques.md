# Wayside — Character Creation Pass 03: Starting Techniques

**Status:** Working proposal — not settled canon  
**Parent spec:** `docs/design/character-and-progression.md`  
**Prior pass:** `docs/design/working/character-creation-pass-02-archetype-stress-test.md`  
**Purpose:** Give each proposed Archetype one concrete starting Technique and test whether the four approaches remain mechanically distinct across multiple weapon families.

> These are prototype-facing starting Techniques. They should be learnable later by characters who began with other Archetypes. They are not permanent exclusive class powers.

---

## 1. Design Rules

Each starting Technique must:

1. Work with melee and ranged weapon families.
2. Avoid assuming a specific armor weight or off-hand.
3. Express the Archetype's core verb rather than a profession or fantasy class.
4. Create a meaningful combat decision rather than a passive statistical bonus.
5. Avoid universal damage multipliers that would make one Archetype mathematically dominant.
6. Remain useful without becoming mandatory.
7. Be understandable early in the game and expandable later through Forms, Augments, or learned Disciplines.

The four working Archetypes remain:

- **Resolute:** hold / commit / impose.
- **Opportunistic:** move / time / exploit.
- **Resourceful:** prepare / combine / create advantage.
- **Perceptive / Adaptive:** read / interpret / respond.

---

## 2. Resolute Starting Technique — Set the Line

### Core function

The player deliberately commits to their current position for a short window. While committed, they become harder to stagger or displace and may carry one weapon action through ordinary interruption. If that action connects, blocks, or otherwise meets resistance, it creates stronger positional pressure rather than simply dealing extra damage.

### Player decision

**Do I give up flexibility for a moment in exchange for reliable commitment?**

The Technique should have a visible commitment cost: reduced movement, limited cancel freedom, or a meaningful recovery if used badly.

### Weapon examples

- **Sword:** hold ground through an incoming clash and complete a committed cut or thrust.
- **Hammer:** finish a heavy swing despite ordinary stagger and force the opponent backward if it lands.
- **Spear:** brace a lane and resist being rushed while delivering a committed thrust.
- **Bow:** maintain the draw or shot under pressure rather than being casually interrupted.
- **Crossbow:** hold a firing position and complete the shot despite ordinary impact pressure.
- **Staff:** brace, redirect pressure, and maintain control of a chokepoint.

### Why it is not the Warrior button

Set the Line does **not** grant:

- permanent damage reduction,
- heavy-armor bonuses,
- exclusive shield behavior,
- increased maximum health,
- universal heavy-weapon scaling.

Its identity is **commitment under pressure**, not toughness as a class stat.

### Failure condition

If it becomes optimal to activate Set the Line before nearly every attack, its cost is too low and it has become a generic damage/poise steroid.

## 3. Opportunistic Starting Technique — Take the Opening

### Core function

When an enemy, hazard, or hostile action enters a clear recovery or exposed state, the player may use a short directional reposition that preserves immediate control rather than functioning as a long invulnerable dodge. The next weapon action made from the new angle receives a situational benefit tied to the opening: cleaner interruption, reduced recovery, improved guard pressure, or another context-appropriate advantage.

The Technique should reward **recognized opportunity**, not raw button speed.

### Player decision

**Is this opening worth repositioning into, and where should I exploit it from?**

### Weapon examples

- **Hammer:** sidestep a missed attack and answer during the opponent's recovery.
- **Spear:** adjust range or angle after forcing an enemy to overextend.
- **Sword and shield:** move around a guarded opponent after their committed strike.
- **Bow:** relocate to a clearer line after an enemy exposes itself.
- **Crossbow:** change angle after a blocked or missed enemy action rather than standing in place.
- **Staff:** slip to a better controlling angle and punish recovery with reach.

### Why it is not the Rogue button

Take the Opening does **not** grant:

- permanent movement-speed bonuses,
- blanket critical-hit chance,
- exclusive stealth access,
- ranged accuracy bonuses,
- light-armor scaling.

Its identity is **timing and positional exploitation**.

### Failure condition

If the Technique is equally strong without an actual opening, it becomes a generic mobility skill and loses the Archetype's conceptual identity.

## 4. Resourceful Starting Technique — Hasty Rig

### Core function

The player rapidly creates one simple temporary battlefield setup using a reusable field kit. The prototype version should create a small non-damaging **hindrance zone** or trip-line-like obstacle that briefly disrupts the first enemy who crosses it.

It does not deal meaningful damage by itself. Its purpose is to manufacture an opening that the player's chosen weapon can exploit.

The kit is reusable after the encounter or automatically recovered; the Technique does not require farming consumables merely to achieve baseline combat parity.

### Player decision

**Where can I change the terms of this fight before the enemy reaches me?**

### Weapon examples

- **Sword:** rig a narrow approach, then engage the enemy when their movement is disrupted.
- **Hammer:** create a predictable stumble and punish it with a committed strike.
- **Spear:** establish a controlled lane where the enemy must cross the rig to close distance.
- **Bow:** use the rig to buy one clean firing window rather than receiving a passive ranged bonus.
- **Crossbow:** prepare a reload-safe zone or force an advancing enemy into an unfavorable approach.
- **Staff:** combine the hindrance with reach and positional control.

### Why it is not the Crafter button

Hasty Rig does **not** require high crafting skill, consume rare materials, increase crafting quality, or unlock a private trap subsystem.

The starting Technique represents **practical setup under pressure**. More elaborate tools may later come from learned Disciplines, Former Trades, recipes, or equipment.

### Failure condition

If every fight begins with the player laboriously placing several devices, Resourceful has become a trap class and combat pacing has suffered.

The starting version should be quick, limited, and tactically meaningful.

---

## 5. Perceptive / Adaptive Starting Technique — Read and Answer

### Core function

The player enters a brief attentive state aimed at one nearby threat. If that threat begins a clearly telegraphed committed action during the window, the player earns a short **response window** in which their chosen defensive or countering action becomes cleaner to execute.

The Technique does not automatically select the correct response. The player still chooses whether to block, evade, reposition, interrupt, or counterattack.

A successful read may reduce the recovery or stamina burden of the chosen response, or briefly improve its ability to convert defense into initiative. Exact numerical effects remain prototype-dependent.

### Player decision

**What is this enemy about to do, and how do I want to answer it?**

### Weapon examples

- **Hammer:** read a vulnerable committed attack and answer with a stagger attempt rather than blindly trading.
- **Sword:** choose between block, evade, or counterthrust after identifying the enemy's commitment.
- **Spear:** read an advance and decide whether to retreat, brace, or intercept.
- **Bow:** read a rush or ranged volley and reposition before taking the next shot.
- **Crossbow:** anticipate pressure and choose whether to fire, disengage, or defend instead of being caught mid-commitment.
- **Staff:** redirect or counter once the enemy's intent becomes clear.

### Why it is not the Mage or Investigator button

Read and Answer does **not** reveal hidden lore, exclusive supernatural truths, exact enemy statistics, guaranteed weaknesses, or required quest information.

It applies equally to ordinary human fighters, animals, environmental hazards, and supernatural threats.

Its identity is **observation followed by adaptive response**.

### Failure condition

If it simply slows time, flashes the correct answer, or exposes enemy data automatically, it removes rather than rewards player judgment.

---

## 6. Distinction Test

The Techniques should create four different questions in the player's head:

| Archetype | Starting Technique | Player question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Resolute | **Set the Line** | Can I afford to commit here and force the situation to respect that commitment? |
| Opportunistic | **Take the Opening** | An opening exists — where and when should I exploit it? |
| Resourceful | **Hasty Rig** | How can I alter the situation so an opening exists on my terms? |
| Perceptive / Adaptive | **Read and Answer** | What is about to happen, and what response best fits it? |

This distinction is the strongest evidence so far that the four-Archetype model is viable.

The sequence is conceptually clean:

- **Resolute creates reliability through commitment.**
- **Opportunistic capitalizes on an opening that already exists.**
- **Resourceful manufactures an opening before or during the engagement.**
- **Perceptive identifies an incoming pattern and adapts to it.**

---

## 7. Cross-Archetype Balance Risks

### Resolute risk: universal safety

If Set the Line lets players ignore too many attacks with little downside, it becomes the obvious safest pick. Commitment must expose the player to being outmaneuvered, mistimed, or punished by attacks that should not be met head-on.

### Opportunistic risk: universal damage optimization

If Take the Opening grants a generic damage multiplier after nearly every enemy action, optimizers will treat Opportunistic as the damage Archetype. Its value should come from position, recovery, and situation-specific conversion rather than blanket DPS.

### Resourceful risk: setup tax

If Hasty Rig is required for Resourceful to achieve ordinary combat competence, the Archetype is secretly weaker. Its normal weapon actions must remain fully functional; the Technique should improve terms rather than compensate for a baseline deficit.

### Perceptive risk: information supremacy

Information is often stronger than damage in an investigation-heavy game. Read and Answer therefore remains a combat-reaction Technique and must not become a universal clue or story-information advantage. Narrative perception should come from attributes, Background, training, investigation, and ordinary player observation.

---

## 8. Recommendation

**Keep all four Archetypes for the next design pass.**

These prototype Techniques successfully separate their gameplay identities without assigning any Archetype ownership of a weapon family, armor category, stealth, crafting, social play, or supernatural access.

The two closest Archetypes — Opportunistic and Perceptive / Adaptive — are now more clearly separated:

- Opportunistic acts **after an opening appears** and converts it into positional advantage.
- Perceptive acts **before or during an incoming commitment** and converts a correct read into a better response.

The distinction should now be tested again through starting Forms or foundational perks. If those pass the same separation test, the four-Archetype structure will be strong enough to consider promotion from working proposal to **Current Direction** in the parent character/progression spec.
