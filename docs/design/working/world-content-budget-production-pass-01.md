# Wayside — World & Content Budget Production Pass 01

**Status:** Working content allocation; not canon.
**Inputs:** all system passes through Magic/Covenant.

## 1. Release map

Production Edition contains **Wayside plus two major regions**. Retain old proper nouns only after an asset/content test; for budgeting they are Region One and Region Two.

| Content unit | Wayside | Region One | Region Two | Total ceiling |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Hub/principal settlement | 1 | 1 | 1 | 3 |
| Small inhabited locations | 0 | 2 | 2 | 4 |
| Major project sites | 2 | 2 | 2 | 6 |
| Substantial investigation/exploration sites | 1 shared/tutorial | 3 | 3 | 7 |
| Roadside micro-sites/incidents | 2 shared approaches | 6 | 6 | 14 authored units; ship target 12 |
| Regional capstone | final shared | 1 | 1 | 2 + final |

Sites should overlap categories where sensible. A ruined crossing can be a project, investigation, combat arena, and later travel node; it is not four locations.

## 2. Cast budget

Target 28 named recurring characters: 6 Wayside core, 9 per region, 2 companions, and 2 cross-regional political/covenant figures. Ten may receive high-depth treatment, including both companions. Ambient roles reuse silhouettes and do not receive personal state machines.

## 3. Encounter and item allocations

- 8 enemy archetypes at ship target; 10 hard ceiling;
- 2 regional variants per applicable archetype, using shared rigs/behaviors;
- 2 bespoke supernatural climax silhouettes; final confrontation must reuse one or use environment/staging;
- 3 major climax encounters at most;
- 4 weapon animation families and 12 gameplay profiles;
- 20–24 weapon variants, 15–18 clothing/armor pieces, 10 utility/off-hand/tools;
- 8 combat Techniques plus up to 3 covenant Techniques and 1 spare only if the total remains 12.

## 4. Dialogue and art units

Budget by authored package, not aspirational word count:

- 10 high-depth NPC packages;
- 18 focused NPC packages;
- 6 project packages with 2–3 outcomes;
- 8 side-story packages maximum;
- 12 micro-story packages target;
- 2 companion chains;
- 4 ending variants built from one epilogue matrix.

Every package must declare required portraits, sprites, locations, effects, audio cues, state flags, QA paths, and localization words before greenlight. A branch that needs a new site, character, and mechanic is a new package, not “just dialogue.”

## 5. Region selection test

Score inherited Blueprint regions on five equally weighted functions: distinct material problem, political contrast, covenant evidence, asset reuse, and ability to host two projects. Select the best pair; merge strong unused people/incidents into them. Current evidence does not justify naming the pair, so Q1 remains Open. No third region may survive as a prologue, dream realm, or endgame corridor in disguise.

## 6. References and philosophy

- **A Short Hike:** borrow dense reuse and memorable authored landmarks; reject its low-conflict content model as a direct template.
- **Bastion:** borrow revisiting a compact hub between bounded levels; reject a linear level parade that makes roads incidental.
- **Death's Door:** borrow a small enemy vocabulary recombined by context; reject its boss/animation emphasis.
- **Roadwarden:** borrow regional economy of recurring routes and text events; adapt them into visual local maps.
- **Design principle — content accounting:** the true unit is the package of writing, art, logic, audio, and QA. Every new noun incurs cross-discipline cost.

## 8. Gates

- **W1 content ledger:** every unit receives multidisciplinary cost fields before production.
- **W2 second-region test:** a paper beat map must achieve distinct material/political/covenant functions without new core systems.
- **W3 reuse test:** at least 60% of Region Two's runtime systems and rigs reuse proven Region One foundations.
- **W4 playtime model:** paper timing must fit 6–8 critical / 10–12 thorough without requiring all side content.

No gate has been executed. Proper nouns and exact node counts below ceilings remain Open.

## 9. Quarantine

Third regions, seasons as content sets, procedural roads, large cities, faction headquarters, expansive Wild realms, repeatable contracts, postgame simulation, and multiplayer maps are North Star/post-release concepts only.
