## 1. Proposal

After prototype gates and Taylor's approval, replace the broad Blueprint with a shorter Production Edition canon assembled from the working package. Preserve the old Blueprint in history; do not edit piecemeal in a way that leaves contradictory scope promises.

Recommended promotion is **conditional**, not immediate: foundational identity and hard ceilings may be approved as paper canon; feel, numeric balance, throughput, and technical-format choices stay Open until evidence exists.

## 3. Explicit high-impact records

### OLD ELEMENT: 35–50-hour game / five-region campaign

**OUTCOME:** Supersede.
**PLAYER PURPOSE:** breadth and long-form journey.
**PRODUCTION TEST:** fails team/content/art/QA ceilings.
**NEW HOME:** Boundaries and World Budget.
**REASON:** two dense regions better preserve meaningful stewardship.
**DEPENDENCIES:** W4 paper timing and later playtest.

### OLD ELEMENT: level 50 and large progression webs

**OUTCOME:** Supersede.
**PLAYER PURPOSE:** long-term growth and build ownership.
**PRODUCTION TEST:** reward/UX/balance volume exceeds a 6–12-hour RPG.
**NEW HOME:** Progression Pass.
**REASON:** aptitudes, familiarities, and learned Techniques make growth legible without filler.
**DEPENDENCIES:** progression slice evidence.

### OLD ELEMENT: six companions and romances

**OUTCOME:** Supersede companions; Defer/Open romance.
**PLAYER PURPOSE:** relational travel and ideological perspectives.
**PRODUCTION TEST:** cast-wide reactivity and party matrices are severe multipliers.
**NEW HOME:** Companions Pass.
**REASON:** two full friendship arcs can receive adequate depth.
**DEPENDENCIES:** A1–A4.

### OLD ELEMENT: four movements and six endings

**OUTCOME:** Replace.
**PLAYER PURPOSE:** competing legitimate futures.
**PRODUCTION TEST:** parallel faction/ending content exceeds budget.
**NEW HOME:** Narrative Pass.
**REASON:** three blocs preserve the philosophical triangle; one synthesis remains earned.
**DEPENDENCIES:** Q2/Q4.

### OLD ELEMENT: bounded simulation of economy, caravans, projects, seasons, schedules

**OUTCOME:** Supersede continuous simulation; Preserve visible causality.
**PLAYER PURPOSE:** a world changed by roads.
**PRODUCTION TEST:** independent simulations create opacity and testing explosion.
**NEW HOME:** event-driven Travel, NPC, Economy, Roadwork, Architecture passes.
**REASON:** shared authored states produce visible consequences at controllable cost.
**DEPENDENCIES:** X2/X5 and system feel gates.

### OLD ELEMENT: painterly world plus ink-and-wash presentation

**OUTCOME:** Preserve identity; Replace allocation.
**PLAYER PURPOSE:** grounded world and expressive storybook interpretation.
**PRODUCTION TEST:** feasible only with tiered portrait/rig/delta caps.
**NEW HOME:** Art Audit.
**REASON:** concentrates expensive art at focal moments.
**DEPENDENCIES:** V1–V5.

## 4. Consolidated Open/evidence register

Prototype-dependent: combat aim/tempo; second weapon set; repair; fatigue/provisions; mechanical weather; map UI; hands-on project intervention; economy values; companion autonomy; investigation clarity; covenant charges; style coherence; rig throughput; content data format; save migration; and full-package authoring throughput.

Paper-open: final region names/merged inherited locations; companion identities; exact three-bloc names; exact covenant wording/hidden inequity; final Technique list; specialty plot; audio production budget; engine point version.

These are not permission for scope expansion. Defaults and cuts in each pass apply if evidence is weak.

## 5. Documents proposed for Production Edition canon source

Use the working passes from Foundation and Boundaries through the Full Integration Audit as source material. At promotion, create one internally consistent `Wayside_Game_Design_Blueprint_Production_Edition_v0.2.md`, update `character-and-progression.md` to remove conflicting active-canon language, and retain this migration table as the decision ledger.

Do not promote:

- untested numerical values as settled;
- optional features beyond targets merely because they fit a ceiling;
- references as proof that Wayside will feel the same;
- any North Star/online feature;
- the old document's contradictory 35–50-hour, five-region, six-companion, level-50, or broad-simulation promises.

## 6. Approval checklist

Taylor's explicit review should decide:

1. whether the compressed product still feels like Wayside;
2. whether three political blocs and four ending variants preserve the desired argument;
3. whether two companions with friendship-first depth are acceptable;
4. whether witness resonance is an acceptable protagonist anchor;
5. whether proper-noun selection can wait for the content map;
6. whether the package may become working canon before or only after slice evidence.

Until that approval, every new document remains under `docs/design/working/`, and the canonical Blueprint remains untouched.
