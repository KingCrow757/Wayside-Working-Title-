# Wayside — Risk & Cut Hierarchy Production Pass 01

**Status:** Working production governance; not canon.
**Dependency consumed:** architecture and vertical-slice plan.

## 1. Risk ranking

| Rank | Risk | Leading indicator | Mandatory response |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cross-system consequence/state explosion | A project outcome requires bespoke checks in 4+ scripts/scenes | Centralize state transaction; reduce reaction package |
| 2 | Content/word/art volume | First full package exceeds ledger estimate by >25% | Cut package count before quality |
| 3 | Combat/animation feel | One family fails readability after two focused iterations | Simplify tempo/input; do not add families |
| 4 | Roadwork feels like menu choice or chores | Players cannot connect investigation/labor/outcome | Strengthen one physical intervention; remove logistics clicks |
| 5 | Travel feels like loading friction | Choices are obvious/repetitive | Shorten transition and author fewer, sharper events |
| 6 | Companion AI/content burden | Requires repeated commands or broad exceptions | Convert to passive + signature cooldown |
| 7 | Hybrid art inconsistency | World and interface feel like separate products | Narrow ink layer and unify palette/symbols |
| 8 | Economy/crafting becomes busywork | Repeated maintenance or profitable grind dominates | Milestone repair; fewer recipes; fixed bands |
| 9 | Tool/architecture overbuilding | Tools precede second proven use | Stop and author second fixture manually/data-first |

## 2. Protected core

Do not cut these without reconsidering the game's identity:

- roads as world structure;
- investigate → travel/prepare → steward project → witness consequence loop;
- one materially and morally distinct project with visible change;
- grounded life beside an uncanny relational covenant;
- multiple valid approaches;
- one recurring Wayside hub;
- reliable controller/keyboard play, saving, readability, and accessibility.

## 3. Cut ladder

Cuts occur in order; each higher tier is considered before a lower tier.

### Tier 1 — invisible complexity

Persistent wear, fatigue, manual map notes, subtle schedules, market refresh detail, ambient conditional lines, cosmetic variants, and low-value inventory distinctions.

### Tier 2 — breadth above target

Spare 12th Technique, weapon/armor variants above targets, side investigations 7–8, micro-stories above 10, third expressions, regional enemy variants, fourth Wayside choice.

### Tier 3 — optional systems/content

Active companion command (retain passive utility), focused NPC portraits, equipment refits beyond four, surrender outside authored encounters, specialty plot selection (retain authored occupant), weather mechanics beyond route-level choices.

### Tier 4 — release structure reduction

Second full companion (retain as major NPC), one major project per region folded into capstone, Region Two inhabited side location, fourth ending synthesis variant.

### Tier 5 — core redesign trigger

If one region, one project type, or non-action combat is required, stop and re-scope the product explicitly. Do not disguise a new core as an ordinary cut.

## 4. Trade rule

Anything added after this audit must name what is removed from the same cost class: writing/QA state, environment art, character art, animation, systems/tooling, or runtime risk. “AI can help” is not a cost estimate and cannot waive review, integration, taste, or testing labor.

## 5. References and philosophy

- **Scope-boxing / fixed resources:** quality and schedule are protected by varying feature breadth, not by treating every idea as mandatory.
- **MVP is not minimum identity:** cut peripheral breadth before the differentiating experience.
- **Sunk-cost discipline:** prototype failure is evidence to simplify even after writing/art investment.
- **Into the Breach:** useful example of depth from bounded rules and enemy combinations; reject using content count as the primary variety source.

## 7. Review cadence

Apply this hierarchy at slice Stage 1, Stage 3, Stage 5, first complete Region One package, content midpoint, and content lock. No calendar duration is invented here. A gate is passed only by recorded build evidence.
