# Wayside — Companions & Relationships Production Pass 01

**Status:** Working proposal; not canon.
**Shared dependencies:** Narrative scope and the 28-character cast target.

## 1. Decision

Ship at most **two full companions**, one active at a time. Each is a person with a worldview, field expertise, combat support profile, and one substantial personal chain. Friendship is the complete launch relationship path. Romance remains outside baseline and may not consume placeholder writing or UI budget.

## 2. Functional pairing

Use complementary design briefs, not final characters:

- **The Local Witness:** knows Region One's communities and material customs; support profile favors protection/control; challenges abstract policy that ignores who bears cost.
- **The Boundary Reader:** understands Region Two's covenant evidence; support profile favors scouting/disruption; challenges both human certainty and romanticized ideas of the Wild.

Names, identities, histories, and exact abilities are Open until regional casts are budgeted. Neither companion owns a required solution; traveling alone remains viable.

## 3. Relationship model

Track four authored states per companion: Reserved, Allied, Strained, and Estranged/Departed. Transitions occur only at major decisions or personal-chain resolutions. There is no visible approval number, gift loop, banter farming, jealousy system, or universal flirt mode.

Each companion budget includes:

- 1 recruitment sequence;
- 3 Wayside conversations;
- 4 regional/project reaction packages;
- 6 short travel/arrival remarks;
- 1 personal chain with 3 major beats and 2 outcomes;
- 1 final policy confrontation/reconciliation;
- 2 epilogue states plus a strained variant.

Reuse shared staging and portrait rigs. Inter-companion material is limited to three authored Wayside scenes; only one companion travels, so no party-banter matrix exists.

## 4. Gameplay boundary

Companions provide passive field expertise, autonomous combat behavior, and at most one contextual signature command. They use fixed equipment silhouettes with at most one visual upgrade. Doctrine selection has no more than three options and occurs at safe preparation points.

Companion absence must not block essential combat, investigation, or project completion. It may change cost, risk, evidence, or tone.

## 5. References and philosophy

- **Pyre:** borrow relationships revealed through travel, disagreement, and consequential futures; reject a large ensemble and combinatorial group scenes.
- **Dragon Age II:** borrow companions with values rather than pure approval and relationships that tolerate principled disagreement; reject romance/party/cross-companion content scale.
- **The Last of Us:** borrow companion utility and presence without constant command; reject cinematic animation/voice requirements.
- **Hades:** borrow economical recurring hub conversations; reject thousands of conditional dialogue events.
- **Design principle — authored sparsity:** fewer reactions, placed after consequential acts, produce stronger perceived memory than abundant generic chatter.

## 6. Migration register

| Old element (§21.1) | Outcome | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Approximately six major companions | Supersede | Two maximum |
| One active companion | Preserve | Core production rule |
| Combat role/doctrine | Replace | One support profile + max three doctrines + one command |
| Personal values and disagreement | Preserve | Four authored relationship states |
| Personal undertaking and ending | Preserve | One three-beat chain and bounded epilogue |
| Inter-companion relationships | Replace | Three shared hub scenes, no matrix |
| Authored romances | Open | Outside launch baseline; no reserved pipeline |
| Equal friendship path | Preserve | Friendship is full relationship baseline |

## 7. Gates

- **A1 usefulness:** combat/site test with each companion and solo. Each changes approach without becoming required.
- **A2 autonomy:** companion must avoid hazards and obey broad doctrine without continuous orders. If unreliable, reduce combat ability to cooldown command plus passive presence.
- **A3 relationship clarity:** players must identify why a state changed from dialogue and journal memory, without points.
- **A4 content cost:** implement one complete project reaction package before writing all eight. If staging/writing exceeds budget, reduce each companion to three project packages.

No gate has been tested.

## 8. Scope exclusions

No party inventory, tactical pause roster, equipment paper-doll, camp-management loop, gift economy, approval grind, romance baseline, player-created companion, companion permadeath simulation, cooperative player slot, or multiplayer party.
