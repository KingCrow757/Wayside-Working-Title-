# Wayside — Magic & Covenant Production Pass 01

**Status:** Working proposal; not canon; mystery text remains provisional.
**Shared dependencies:** Narrative politics, investigations, projects, combat techniques, and content budget.

## 1. Decision

Magic remains **relational, prepared, and costly in meaning rather than mana throughput**. The covenant is both the world's central mystery and the ruleset connecting roads, communities, and the Wild. Production Edition supports household observances, site-specific workings, and at most **three player-usable covenant Techniques** within the existing 8–12 total Technique budget.

There is no standalone spell school, caster class, mana bar, or parallel magical crafting economy.

## 2. Covenant foundation

Working historical model:

- Earlier settlements received safe passage and bounded habitation through a compact witnessed at road anchors.
- Human communities owed maintenance, seasonal restraint, and renewed local consent—not permanent surrender of all land.
- Later authorities simplified reciprocal stewardship into a claim of perpetual human right, while expansion and neglected renewal overloaded the anchors.
- The Wild is plural: some claim restitution, some continuity, and some the collapse of all human roads.
- Failure is occurring now because accumulated breaches coincide with the last intact renewal circuit losing credible witnesses.

This supplies a playable causal spine without fully defining the oldest beings or making one faction objectively correct. Exact original wording, the identity of the first concealment, and whether any nonhuman community bore a hidden unequal cost remain **Open M1** until narrative prototypes test clarity and thematic weight.

## 3. Player capability

The protagonist's sensitivity is **witness resonance**: they can perceive contradictory marks because they survived contact with a failing roadside anchor before arrival. This is an authored inciting fact, not chosen-one bloodline or unlimited magic license. Background changes interpretation and contacts, not the power source.

Three candidate Techniques, all counted inside Progression's total:

- **Hold the Line:** prepared ward that briefly protects a boundary/ally;
- **Turn the Mark:** redirect one marked hazard or hostile working;
- **Call Witness:** reveal a concealed covenant relation/evidence during an authored context.

Exact combat utility and resource/cost are **Open M2**. Prototype tokens/charges against cooldown-only use. If preparation becomes inventory busywork, use rest-prepared charges without craft inputs.

## 4. Major workings

Each major project may include one covenant decision with 2–3 explicit terms. The UI states the broad obligation before commitment and records it in Promises. Consequences may reveal incomplete understanding, but never invent an undisclosed category of price.

Only the final project may combine multiple prior terms. No procedural contract generator exists; every bargain and breach is authored.

## 5. Visual and content grammar

Reuse marks, boundary lines, knots, witness stones, displaced sound, and ink distortion. Effects prioritize shape and timing over particle density. Covenant beings reuse a small rig/effect vocabulary; the world budget may allocate no more than two bespoke major supernatural encounter silhouettes.

## 6. References and philosophy

- **The Black Company / folkloric bargains:** borrow magic whose danger comes from relationships and terms; reject unlimited spectacle as routine utility.
- **Dishonored:** borrow powers that interact with spaces and approaches; reject a broad power tree and chaos simulation.
- **Pyre:** borrow rites as social obligation and judgment; reject a separate ritual-sport ruleset.
- **Darkwood:** borrow the uncanny emerging from grounded materials and spaces; reject pervasive horror and opaque punishment.
- **Sanderson's First Law as a design check:** only magic whose rules the player can understand should solve gameplay problems; mystery may remain in origin and motive.

## 8. Gates

- **M1 mystery clarity:** readers/players can state the known compact, the contested history, and what remains unknown.
- **M2 magic feel:** compare prepared charges, situational refresh, and cooldown-only use in one combat/site scene.
- **M3 bargain consent:** players predict the category of consequence before agreeing, while outcomes remain nontrivial.
- **M4 visual readability:** marks remain legible at camera scale, in weather, and with reduced effects.
- **M5 content reuse:** a second covenant site must reuse the grammar without feeling like the same puzzle.

No gate has been tested.

## 9. Scope exclusions

No elemental spell list, spellcraft combinator, mana economy, summon roster, procedural bargains, morality corruption meter, chosen-one genealogy, full Wild bestiary civilization sim, or online covenant factions.
