# Wayside — Settlement Production Pass 01

**Status:** Working consolidation; not canon.
**Dependencies consumed:** NPC/community consequences and Crafting/Economy.

## 1. Settlement role

Wayside is a **recurring authored home that consolidates relationships, preparation, and visible consequence**. It is not a settlement builder. Its fixed crossroads layout gains a small number of authored scene swaps and operator changes across three story phases.

## 2. Functional footprint

Seven functional places are the release maximum:

1. common room/inn — rest, news, companion scenes;
2. yard/stores — project ledger, deliveries, travel preparation;
3. workshop — repair/refit and infrastructure expertise;
4. infirmary — recovery and medicine;
5. archive/map room — journal, cartography, covenant research;
6. training ground — technique preparation and practice;
7. flexible roadside plot — one selected specialty.

Functions may share a building or scene. “Seven” is an interaction budget, not seven bespoke interiors.

## 3. Player choices

Wayside supports **four meaningful choices** across the game:

- one of three specialties for the flexible plot;
- workshop emphasis: field logistics or equipment refit;
- archive emphasis: route intelligence or covenant interpretation;
- final public identity reflecting the chosen regional/covenant policy.

The first three can be repurposed once at a clear coin/time cost; relationship and historical effects remain. Essential rest, repair, storage, and route access cannot be locked out. These four choices fit within the 3–5 settlement-choice ceiling; only two carry major-project consequence weight.

## 4. Visual change budget

Use three base phases—worn, functioning, established—with layered swaps for signs, traffic, repaired surfaces, one specialty structure, and two regional material accents. Cap the settlement at **12 authored visual deltas** beyond base phases. A delta should be visible from a commonly used route or inside a recurring service scene.

Regional outcomes send people, goods, and visual traces to Wayside. They do not spawn new simulation systems. Ambient population density changes through reused sprites and placement sets.

## 5. Services and surplus

Wayside stores secured project cargo and personal possessions in distinct ledgers. Its capped surplus is a milestone-granted allowance of provisions/repair support, not passive currency. Operators provide the compact economy catalogue. No building produces items on a timer and no player must harvest output.

## 6. References and philosophy

- **Hades:** borrow a compact hub repeatedly recontextualized by dialogue and small visual changes; reject its run-cycle and enormous reactive script volume.
- **Darkest Dungeon:** borrow clear facility function and upgrades that change preparation; reject tier ladders, roster management, and economy pressure.
- **Pillars of Eternity (Caed Nua):** borrow ownership and visible restoration; reject remote management, taxes, random attacks, and a broad upgrade tree.
- **Spiritfarer:** borrow the emotional value of a changing home; reject free placement and a platforming/production layout puzzle.
- **Design principle — hub density:** every return should answer “what changed, who is here, what can I prepare?” without requiring a maintenance circuit.

## 7. Migration register

| Old element (§11 unless noted) | Outcome | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Curated settlement customization | Preserve | Fixed layout plus authored choices |
| Limited plots and named operators | Replace | One flexible plot; other functions fixed/shared |
| Restrained cosmetic materials/signs/banners/gardens | Replace | Consequence-led visual deltas; no cosmetic picker baseline |
| Six potential advanced facilities | Supersede | Seven fixed/shared functions and three emphasis choices |
| Capped automatic surplus | Preserve | Milestone allowance, never timer income |
| Repurpose at meaningful cost | Preserve | One repurpose per emphasis/specialty |
| General settlement building | Open | Explicitly outside launch; no prototype scheduled |

## 8. Gates

- **S1 return-loop test:** three returns must each expose change, relationship, and preparation within two minutes without a chore circuit.
- **S2 navigation test:** controller users reach every recurring service quickly; consolidate scenes if traversal becomes dead time.
- **S3 art delta test:** build worn/functioning variants of one exterior. If swaps are not legible at gameplay scale, prioritize traffic/signage and drop subtle decoration.
- **S4 choice-value test:** each emphasis must alter at least two later decisions. Otherwise convert it to authored progression and remove the false choice.

These gates are untested; exact building arrangement and specialty identity remain Open pending region/narrative content.

## 9. Scope exclusions

No free placement, residents-as-workers assignment, raids, happiness score, resource production chains, décor catalogue, housing, romance domicile, multiplayer visitors, or live-service expansion hooks.
